About

Rows are 攝 (Roman: the 12 攝 of the rhyme tables; Arabic: Beaudouin's 8). In each 攝, the lax (major-cycle) rhymes come first, with the second-column rhymes shaded below the dashed line; tense (minor cycle 1) and rhotic (minor cycles 2 and 3) rhymes sit on the row of their lax counterpart. A rhyme split over two rows has one reading per row.

GHC, GX and Miy are as in the table compiled for this site. All other columns, and Beaudouin's analysis (initial, medial, phonation), are from Mathieu Beaudouin, Grammaire du tangoute. Phonologie et morphologie, thèse, INALCO, 2023, “Reconstruction des rimes du tangoute”, a first draft that he expects to revise. His MB column is the PHONO column there: ʶ = uvularised (grades I and II), ʕ with -ɹ- = grade II, ˞ = rhoticised (minor cycle 2), a̯(C-) = pre-vocalised syllable (minor cycle 1), Cː- = geminate initial (second column).

Sources of the reconstructions: Nishida Tatsuo 西田龍雄, 西夏語の研究 (1964–1966); Michail V. Sofronov, Грамматика тангутского языка (1968), two stages of the language; Gong Hwang-cherng 龔煌城, “The Phonological Reconstruction of Tangut through Examination of Phonological Alternations”, BIHP 60(1), 1989; Gong Xun (several works; Tangut phonology, 2024); Arakawa Shintarō 荒川慎太郎 & Evgenij Kychanov, Словарь тангутского (Си Ся) языка (2006); Marc Miyake, Amritas (2006).
