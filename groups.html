About

Pairs of characters are compared by three criteria, in this order: sound decides which pairs are compared at all, meaning decides which of them are linked, and character raises the score of a link (it is not required, since only about half of the attested verb stem pairs share a radical run). The other two settings require all three, or group by sound and character alone, which mostly brings out series of characters sharing a component and a reading.

1. Sound (GHC readings; Gong Hwang-cherng, “Phonological Alternations in Tangut”, 1988; a pair is only compared further when this holds). Initials: the same, or alternating as in Gong: voiced ~ voiceless aspirated (b ~ pʰ, l ~ lʰ, ɣ ~ x) or unaspirated ~ aspirated (p ~ pʰ). Finals: the same rhyme; or another rhyme of the same 攝: a change of grade (medial), of length, or of cycle (lax ~ tense ~ rhotic); or a change of 攝 found in Gong's examples or the verb stem pairs (), weighing more when the very same two rhymes are attested (shown as R.x ~ R.y). Tone and -w- may also differ. Each change lowers the weight.

2. Character (Downes's radical codes): the runs of two or more radicals the two codes share inside a block (a hyphen-separated part), weighted by how rare each run is, as a share of the smaller code; single shared radicals count a little. Shared runs are marked in the codes.

3. Meaning: the closest pair of senses in the two glosses, English or Chinese, by cosine in ConceptNet Numberbatch 17.06 word embeddings (the same sense counts 1). Transcription notes and surnames are not senses, so characters glossed only that way have no meaning score.

A link counts for a criterion at . Groups are formed from the strongest links first; two groups are joined only when every pair across them is phonetically related and at least half of those pairs are links of the chosen kind.

Characters are the 5,934 placed in the rhyme tables (Wenhai and non-Wenhai). Built by tools/build_groups.py.
