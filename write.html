How to use

Radicals: type a code (hyphens optional) or click radicals in the table; characters whose code, or one of its parts, starts that way are listed. Reading: GHC in plain letters, with or without tone (dzjwo2, dzjwo; ɨ/ə as i, e or y; several syllables for a word). Meaning: English or Chinese. Tangut: paste text; it is split into dictionary words.

Enter adds the highlighted result at the cursor · ↑↓ choose · with the box empty, ←→ move the cursor and ⌫ deletes · Alt+1–4 switch search. In the sentence: click to place the cursor, drag to reorder, Shift+←→ moves a word, ① ② switches between a character's readings, double-click a gloss to write your own.

Characters, words, readings (GHC, GX, Miyake), meanings and radical codes are from the Tangut Character Dictionary v2.2 (codes: Alan Downes, Xixia Dictionaries, 2008); MB readings from Mathieu Beaudouin's Digital Wenhai. Drafts are kept in this browser only.
