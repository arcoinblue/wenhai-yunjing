    About

    Each cell shows one 小韻 (the first in Wenhai order); click it to see every 小韻 placed in that cell, with fanqie, all characters, and readings.

    Sections: 平開 tone 1 without -w-, 上開 tone 2 without -w-, 平合 tone 1 with -w-, 上合 tone 2 with -w-. Rows (等) follow GHC's medials: 一 no medial i/j, 二 -i-, 三 -j-; 小韻 that fall together share a cell. Charts are ordered by 攝; within a 攝: plain (本), tense (緊), retroflex (捲), long (長), long retroflex (長捲).

    Placement uses Miyake's rhyme, grade, initial and medial; tone comes from the part of the Wenhai the entry is in. Tone 2 comes only from the 雜類 (the 上聲 part of the Wenhai is lost).

    Sources: Wenhai entries and MB reconstruction: Mathieu Beaudouin, The Digital Wenhai: A Unicode-Compliant Dataset of the Backbone of Tangut Linguistics and Philology (Zenodo). GHC = Gong Hwang-cherng's reconstruction. Marc Miyake, Tangut Database ver. 4.0 (2019). Character meanings: Tangut characters by group v2.0. Tangut fonts: Noto Serif Tangut and BabelStone Tangut Wenhai (Andrew West), both SIL Open Font License, bundled; Tangut Yinchuan (Jing Yongshi / Andrew West), used from your computer or your own copy of the file.
