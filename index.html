    About

    Each cell shows one 小韻 (the first in Wenhai order); click it to see every 小韻 placed in that cell, with fanqie, all characters, and readings.

    Sections: 平開 tone 1 without -w-, 上開 tone 2 without -w-, 平合 tone 1 with -w-, 上合 tone 2 with -w-. Rows are Miyake's grades I–IV. Charts are ordered by 攝; within a 攝: plain (本), tense (緊), retroflex (捲), long (長), long retroflex (長捲).

    Placement uses Miyake's rhyme, grade, initial and medial; tone comes from the part of the Wenhai the entry is in. Tone 2 comes only from the 雜類 (the 上聲 part of the Wenhai is lost).

    Sources: Wenhai entry data (spreadsheet); GHC = Gong Hwang-cherng's reconstruction; Marc Miyake, Tangut Database ver. 4.0 (2019). Tangut font: Noto Serif Tangut (SIL OFL).
