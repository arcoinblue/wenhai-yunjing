文海

The Tangut Wenhai

The Tangut rhyme dictionary 文海 laid out as rhyme tables, with its readings, rhymes and verb stems, and tools for reading and writing Tangut.

  1. Rhyme tablesWeb韻圖 Yunjing-style tables of the Wenhai's 小韻, with GHC, MB and Miyake readings and the page scans.
  2. Rhyme reconstructions The 105 rhymes by 攝, lax ~ tense ~ rhotic, with GHC, Beaudouin and other reconstructions side by side.
  3. Verb stem pairs Verbs with a stem A and a stem B, grouped by how the final alternates.
  4. Radical code practice See a character and type its radical code (Downes's recursive radical method).
  5. Write Tangut Build sentences by radical code, reading or meaning, with the reading and gloss under each word.
