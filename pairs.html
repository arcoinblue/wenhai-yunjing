About

Every character whose gloss is marked [ᴀ] or [ʙ] in Tangut characters by group v2.0, paired by meaning and checked against the readings (same initial, alternating final). Groups are named by the GHC final with medials and vowel diacritics set aside; each row shows the exact readings.

Wenhai marks characters found in the Wenhai, with their position. GHC is Gong Hwang-cherng's reconstruction (as on the rhyme tables); MB is from Mathieu Beaudouin's Digital Wenhai and is blank for characters it does not have; Miyake is Tangut Database ver. 4.0 (2019).

A pair marked ? is tentative. Forms with no partner are listed at the end.
