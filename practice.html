About

Each character's code lists its radicals from top left to bottom right; hyphens separate the structural parts. Codes, the 176 radicals and their pictures are from Alan Downes, Xixia Dictionaries (supplement to The Xixia Writing System, 2008), as included in the Tangut Character Dictionary v2.2; readings and meanings are from the same dictionary.

Keys: Enter check, then next · Tab one more radical as a hint · Esc show the answer.
